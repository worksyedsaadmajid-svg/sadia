About Dr Sadia Mumtaz Hosseiyn

Dr Sadia Mumtaz Hosseiyn is a qualified Dental Surgeon (BDS, MS, RDS, C-ORTHO, C-IMPLANT) and experienced Consultant Orthodontist & Implantologist. She completed her BDS from Pioneer & Best Dental College, KMDC, and is a position holder of Dow University of Health Sciences (DUHS) with the highest GP in her class. She is also involved in PhD studies and dental research, including Oral Cancer and research on good and bad fat among the Pakistani population. Her expertise includes Gingivectomy, Alveoloplasty, Impaction Surgery, Osseous Surgery, Dental Implants, Bone Augmentation, orthodontic attachments, and advanced dental and gum surgical procedures.
